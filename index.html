Profile Summary

I am a public health researcher at icddr,b with more than 10 years of experience in antimicrobial resistance (AMR), antimicrobial stewardship, infection prevention and control (IPC) and One Health, in both community and healthcare settings. I coordinate four research projects across 10 sites with a team of 14, and work as Co-Principal Investigator on externally funded AMR and IPC research. I use quantitative, qualitative and mixed methods, and I have authored 25 peer-reviewed articles.

My research expertise covers quantitative and qualitative research in both community and healthcare settings. I am proficient in developing manuscripts, systematic reviews and meta-analyses, skilled in project implementation, data management and analysis, and experienced in coordinating diverse and large research teams.

Projects

Integrating hospital wastewater AMR surveillance with antibiotic prescribing data

Lead

A pilot study that combines culture-based hospital wastewater surveillance with prescribing analytics, to test feasibility for a longer study.

RoleLead
StatusPilot study
SettingTertiary hospitals, Bangladesh

I designed and carried out this pilot independently. It tests whether wastewater data, linked with antibiotic prescribing records, can track resistance across a whole hospital and inform a PhD research plan.

Strengthening infection prevention and control and antimicrobial stewardship in tertiary hospitals

Co-Principal Investigator

Multimodal IPC and stewardship interventions to improve practice in hospital wards and critical care units.

RoleCo-Principal Investigator
StatusOngoing · Phase II
SettingCritical care units, tertiary hospitals

The project tested a WHO-aligned multimodal IPC package and measured adherence among healthcare workers and family caregivers. Phase II continues the work in critical care units.
