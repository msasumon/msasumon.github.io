Profile Summary

I am a public health researcher at icddr,b with more than 10 years of experience in antimicrobial resistance (AMR), antimicrobial stewardship, infection prevention and control (IPC) and hospital epidemiology in Bangladesh. I lead and coordinate multicentre studies in hospital and community settings, using both quantitative and qualitative methods. I am currently leading a pilot study that links hospital wastewater AMR surveillance with antibiotic prescribing data.
